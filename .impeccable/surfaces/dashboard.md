# Decision dashboard surface

## Confirmed surface brief

The dashboard is an Operate surface for a time-limited portfolio review. A recruiter or business leader should identify the evidence boundary, the decision supported, and the next action in the first viewport. Five views cover summary, acquisition, journeys, integration health, and synthetic scenarios. The design must never make old public sample data look current, synthetic results look observed, or descriptive attribution look causal. Dense evidence must remain navigable by keyboard and understandable without charts.

## Direction evaluation

The grounded set was: executive evidence dossier; data-newsroom investigation wall; scientific protocol notebook; investment research terminal; transit operations board; election-night decision desk; museum archive catalog. Seed `0bf76d39` assigned the sixth direction. Cloud quarry, sneaker archive, PC-98, streaming wall, and seven-segment challengers were declined on both audience identification and product clarity; their strongest disciplines—spatial hierarchy, consistent labels, fixed regions, focus disclosure, and legible states—raise the chosen direction. The emission-line rail was competitive on evidence clarity but weaker for executive familiarity; its disciplined use of state beyond color is retained.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A calm decision-night tally desk: evidence is counted, called, held, or investigated in public view. It refuses the generic SaaS card quilt and the equally generic empty-white spreadsheet.

**OWN-WORLD:** Ink navy and warm paper, ruled tally lines, precise sans type, tabular numerals, observed mint and synthetic violet labels reinforced by words and border patterns. Flat surfaces, square shoulders, no decorative gradients.

**STORY:** Read the recommended move, check evidence strength, inspect the finding, then open its accessible record or move to the relevant decision view.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A narrow masthead and route rail sit above one oversized decision call. Beside it, a horizontal evidence tally shows funnel progression at operational scale; below, two clearly labeled evidence lanes expose public and synthetic work without blending them. The primary action is the first route-specific evidence link.

**FORM:** Election-night decision desk, sixth in the ordered grounded set, seed `0bf76d39`. Signature interaction: the active route becomes the called result while keyboard focus exposes a matching decision note; motion is one restrained tally-line reveal and disappears under reduced motion.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
